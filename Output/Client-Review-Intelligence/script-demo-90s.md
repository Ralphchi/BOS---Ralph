# Script vidéo démo — 90s

**Objectif :** un asset sales à intégrer dans chaque email/DM outreach. Un email EN avec démo = 3× taux de réponse vs sans.

**Format :** screen recording + webcam picture-in-picture (Ralph + frère, bottom-right)
**Durée cible :** 60-90 secondes
**Langue :** EN (brand phase 1 = 100% EN)
**Tournage :** 2 prises maximum, pas de sur-production

---

## Setup technique

- Screen recording Mac (QuickTime ou Loom)
- Webcam : Ralph au centre, frère à côté ou derrière
- Lumière naturelle (face à la fenêtre)
- Fond : neutre et propre, pas de bibliothèque cliché
- Audio : AirPods ou micro externe (pas le micro intégré)

---

## Script (90s)

### [0:00–0:08] Hook

**À l'écran :** Ralph + frère en face caméra

> « Hi, I'm Ralph Chidiac. I'm an EPFL engineering student and with my brother, we build AI for Swiss wealth managers. In 90 seconds, here's what we've built. »

---

### [0:08–0:20] Le pain

**À l'écran :** toujours face caméra

> « A Swiss EAM advisor spends 3 to 4 hours preparing each quarterly client review. For a book of 50 clients, that's 200 hours per quarter — almost 30 full workdays a year spent on manual commentary. »

---

### [0:20–1:05] Demo (screen recording prend le dessus)

**À l'écran :** Claude Project avec le master prompt + le dataset Perrin qui apparaît.

> « Here's our system running on a fictional CHF 4-million client portfolio. It ingests the portfolio snapshot, the quarter's news context, and two commentary samples to learn the firm's tone. »

*[Pause pendant que l'output se génère à l'écran — scroll lentement]*

> « In under two minutes, the advisor gets the executive summary, a detailed performance review, market themes tied specifically to this client's holdings, the outlook section, plus a meeting agenda and — and this is the key — advisor-only talking points the client never sees. All in the firm's own voice. »

---

### [1:05–1:30] CTA

**À l'écran :** retour face caméra, Ralph parle

> « We're running a 30-day pilot at CHF 2,400 with 3 Swiss EAMs in Q2. You run it on 20 of your real clients, we handle the setup, and if the time savings don't hit 50%, we refund. If you're curious, 15 minutes next week is all it takes. Link below. Ralph. »

---
